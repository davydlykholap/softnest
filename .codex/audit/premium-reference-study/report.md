# SoftNest premium-minimal reference study

## Scope

Comparative visual audit of the current SoftNest hero and three user-provided references. The goal is to extract shared design intent without copying layouts, content, imagery, or brand expression.

## Step 1 — SoftNest current hero

Health: Good foundation, but still reads as a polished service-site template rather than a confident modern brand.

- Strong: warm palette, clear emotional headline, excellent negative space, relevant furniture imagery.
- Weak: crowded navigation, large floating header, decorative shadow, two similarly weighted boxed CTAs, visually busy full-colour logo, and a slightly theatrical display serif.

## Step 2 — Vercel reference

Health: Excellent hierarchy and focus.

- Intent: one dominant proposition, decisive contrast, sparse controls, generous emptiness, and proof placed quietly at the edge of the experience.
- Transferable principle: make one element dominant and make everything else deliberately quiet.
- Do not transfer: black technology aesthetic, glowing diagram, enterprise logos, or three-column composition.

## Step 3 — OpenAI reference

Health: Excellent restraint and task focus.

- Intent: navigation recedes, the central action has abundant breathing room, surfaces use minimal decoration, and content below the fold is allowed to provide visual richness.
- Transferable principle: reduce visual framing and let the core action carry the screen.
- Do not transfer: chatbot input metaphor, dark theme, or content-card treatment.

## Step 4 — Anthropic reference

Health: Excellent editorial authority.

- Intent: large typography, asymmetric grid, a restrained cream/black palette, one strong contrast block, and almost no decorative UI.
- Transferable principle: build authority through type, proportion, and spacing rather than effects.
- Do not transfer: institutional AI language, exact headline structure, underlined words, or black media slab.

## Shared design intent

1. One idea dominates the first screen.
2. Large areas of intentional empty space.
3. Very small colour palette with clear contrast roles.
4. Typography establishes hierarchy before containers or shadows do.
5. One primary action; secondary actions recede.
6. Navigation is compact and structurally simple.
7. Proof appears quietly after the main proposition.

## Recommended SoftNest direction

### Brand expression

- Keep warm bone, charcoal, and one deep eucalyptus green.
- Simplify the logo lockup visually: reduce the icon size and saturation; use a near-monochrome version where possible.
- Preserve the emotional headline `Come Home to Clean.` but use a calmer contemporary serif with less extreme stroke contrast.

### Header

- Keep the requested pill silhouette, but reduce it to 64–70px tall with a fine border and barely visible shadow.
- Navigation: `Services`, `Areas`, `Results`, `About`.
- Keep the phone number but reduce its emphasis.
- Use one compact pill CTA: `Free Quote`.

### Hero

- Use a clean asymmetric grid: copy occupies roughly 38–42%; interior photography occupies the rest.
- Short kicker: `Upholstery & carpet care · Toronto & GTA`.
- Keep one short supporting sentence.
- Primary CTA: full green pill with a restrained 4–6px cream/sage ring.
- Secondary CTA: quiet text link or lightly outlined pill, not equal visual weight.
- Add only one proof line beneath: `Free photo estimate · No obligation`.

### Image direction

- Keep authentic residential materials: boucle, wool, linen, oak, muted olive, natural daylight.
- Prefer a single believable sofa-and-rug scene with visible fabric texture and slight lived-in imperfection.
- Avoid staged cleaner poses, floating badges, artificial glow, excessive white haze, or showroom-perfect symmetry.
- Show cleaning process and before/after evidence below the hero, not inside it.

### Type and spacing

- UI/body direction: neutral grotesk such as Geist or Inter.
- Headline direction: restrained editorial serif such as Instrument Serif or Source Serif 4.
- Headline desktop target: 72–88px, line-height about 0.95–1.0, maximum two lines.
- Body: 18–20px with 1.45–1.55 line-height.
- Use a consistent 8px spacing system and a wide 12-column grid.

### Below the fold

- Replace trust-card clusters with one quiet verified-proof rail.
- Present services as an editorial numbered list rather than icon cards.
- Use one large real-results section with generous space and direct labels.
- Motion should be subtle: 200–300ms fades, understated underlines, and no bouncing or glow effects.

## Accessibility and evidence limits

- Static screenshots do not verify font files, exact colour contrast, responsive reflow, semantic structure, keyboard focus, or motion preferences.
- In implementation, confirm 4.5:1 text contrast where required, visible focus states, minimum 44px targets, and readable mobile line lengths.
