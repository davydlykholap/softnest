# SoftNest hero audit

## Scope

Single desktop homepage hero screenshot. User goal: understand how to improve hierarchy, professionalism, and conversion while preserving the soft interior direction.

## Step 1 — Homepage entry

Health: Strong foundation; refinement needed.

### Strengths

- Distinctive, calm interior image with useful negative space for the copy.
- Strong emotional headline with effective black/green emphasis.
- Clear primary and secondary actions.
- Pill header is memorable and matches the furniture geometry.

### Highest-impact improvements

1. Consolidate navigation to Services, Areas, Results, and About. Group Reviews and Before & After under Results; move FAQ to the footer or Services menu.
2. Make both hero CTAs true pills. Give the primary CTA a restrained cream/sage outer ring; keep the secondary button outlined.
3. Use title case for buttons and navigation for a more polished, approachable corporate voice.
4. Reduce the header height and shadow slightly, and lower the visual weight of the phone number.
5. Move the hero content modestly upward and reduce the headline size by about 5% to improve vertical balance.
6. Add one quiet reassurance line under the CTAs: Free photo estimate · No obligation.

### Accessibility risks and limits

- Confirm text and outline contrast in code; the image alone cannot establish exact contrast ratios.
- Add visible keyboard focus states and at least 44px interaction targets.
- Responsive reflow, hover behavior, semantics, and keyboard navigation cannot be verified from a static screenshot.
